How the numbers are worked out

Loan interest is charged every month on what you still owe, the same way a bank does it. A SIP's return is the rate you type in. It is a what-if, not a forecast, and past returns do not promise future ones.

Where tax applies, it shows up as its own line so you can see how much it takes. Each page also says what it leaves out, such as stamp duty or exit loads.

These tools are for learning and comparing. They do not recommend any fund, stock or lender, and they are no substitute for a registered adviser.
