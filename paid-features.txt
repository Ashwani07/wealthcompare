2. THE OPTIONS (NONE CHOSEN)
----------------------------
A. Display ads (AdSense). Already set up. Depends on approval, which depends
   on the amount and quality of original content.
B. [PAUSED] A small one-off paid extra. Earlier idea: a one-time unlock of roughly
   ₹99 to 149 to save and export a scenario as PDF, through a payment link,

   not been tested.
C. [PAUSED] Premium features (section 5), which would need accounts at some point.
D. Referral or affiliate links (for example a balance transfer or a demat
   account). NOT in the plan. They sit close to product recommendations,
   which the site's educational-only rule forbids. Get advice from a
   SEBI-aware lawyer or compliance professional before considering any.
E. [PAUSED] Email capture or lead generation. NOT in the plan. It brings consent and
   notice duties under India's data protection law. Take legal advice first.

Ads and paid features do not exclude each other, but they affect the same
visitors. Decide which one comes first and do not build payment code in
advance of a decision.

4. WHAT THE FREE TOOLS ALREADY DO
---------------------------------
Premium ideas must build on this, not hide it.
- Five tools: EMI Calculator, Loan vs SIP, SIP Growth, Loan Prepayment and
  Step-Up SIP Goal. The first is on the older layout.
- Each reworked tool shows the verdict first, has a collapsed full breakdown
  and an Advanced Read, validates inputs inline, and exports to Excel.
- Results are shown in today's rupees as well as future rupees, with real and
  nominal kept consistent on both sides.
- SIP gains use 12.5% above ₹1,25,000, applied once at redemption
  (Section 198, Income-tax Act 2025). Units held 12 months or less would be
  taxed at 20% (Section 196); the free tools state this but do not model it.
- Loan vs SIP includes selling cost and tax on the property's sale gain as
  inputs, a loan-to-value warning against the RBI limits, and the assumption
  that the visitor already owns a home to live in.
- Year-by-year tables are free on SIP Growth, Step-Up SIP Goal and Loan
  Prepayment.
- Loan Prepayment (lump sums, monthly extra, rate change, reduce EMI versus
  reduce tenure) is free.
- Colours come from shared CSS variables, CDN scripts are pinned, and no
  tool shows NaN or Infinity.

6. PRODUCT RULES
----------------
- Show every assumption beside the result and offer a reset to defaults.
- Never present a projection as a guaranteed return or as personal advice.
- Never recommend a specific fund, stock, lender, broker or product.
- Validate numeric inputs and explain errors inline. Premium inputs follow the
  same pattern as the free ones.
- Keep calculations reproducible: record inputs, formula version and tax year.
- Collect no sensitive financial information unless it is strictly needed.
  Before adding payments, accounts, email capture or referral links, check the
  legal duties (data protection, consumer rules, SEBI) with a qualified
  person.
- Give clear value before asking for payment, and never interrupt the
  calculation flow.


7. SEO AND CONTENT NOTES
------------------------
- One descriptive H1 per page and a unique title and meta description.
- Every tool page has Open Graph and Twitter tags and a WebApplication
  JSON-LD block. Articles use an Article block.
- Link related pages in both directions: EMI, Loan Prepayment, Loan vs SIP,
  SIP Growth, Step-Up, and the articles that explain them.
- Show a visible published or last-updated date on articles, and update it
  when a tax rule or assumption changes.
- Do not publish many near-identical pages. Fewer, better pages with their own
  analysis suit search engines and AdSense reviewers better.
- Write articles as worked examples with a stated assumption table, results
  in today's rupees and future rupees, and a "what changes the answer"
  section. Every number comes from a calculator or a script.
