About WealthCompare

Why this site exists

Most loan and SIP calculators show you a big final number and stop there. WealthCompare goes one step further. It shows what that number is worth after inflation and, where it applies, after tax, so you compare what the money can actually buy and not just how big the digits look.

A crore twenty years from now will not buy what a crore buys today. Every tool here says so in rupees you can picture.

Who is behind it

WealthCompare is built and maintained independently by one person based in India. It is not a bank, a fund house or a licensed advisory, and it is not a company. It is a small, self-funded project: one person writing the tools and checking the maths.

Rather than ask you to trust a name, the aim is to earn trust by showing the working. Every assumption is written next to the result it affects. Every email sent to the address below is read and answered by the person who built the tools.

The tools

There are five calculators so far: EMI Calculator, Loan vs SIP, SIP Growth, Loan Prepayment and Step-Up SIP Goal. The articles under Insights explain the thinking behind them with worked examples.

How the numbers are checked

Every assumption, such as inflation, expected return and tax rate, is a plain input you control. Nothing is hidden inside the maths. Each tool has a collapsible "Advanced Read" that explains the method and lists what it leaves out.

The calculators are built from a spreadsheet model. Each page is tested against that model with many sets of inputs, including awkward ones such as a zero interest rate or the longest allowed tenure, and the pages must give the same numbers. Key figures are also recalculated with a separate method.

Tax and regulatory figures are checked against the Income Tax Department, RBI and SEBI websites before they are used, and each article lists its sources and the date they were checked. When there is no official source for a claim, the page says so. Rules change, so please confirm current figures before you act on them.

What this site is not

WealthCompare is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser, research analyst or distributor. It does not give personalised financial advice and does not recommend any fund, stock, lender or broker. It is an educational tool to help you think through trade-offs before you talk to your bank, a SEBI-registered investment adviser or a chartered accountant.

How the site is paid for

The tools and articles are free. The site may show advertising to cover its costs, and paid extras may be added later. Ads and paid features never change a number or what a tool says.

Get in touch

Questions, corrections, or a bug you have spotted in a calculation are all welcome. If a number looks wrong, tell us the inputs you used and we will check it against the model.
