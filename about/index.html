About WealthCompare

Why this site exists

Most loan and SIP calculators show you a big final number and stop there. WealthCompare exists to go one step further: showing what that number is actually worth after inflation and, where relevant, tax — so you're comparing real purchasing power, not just impressive-looking digits.

Who's behind it

WealthCompare is built and maintained independently by a solo software developer based in India — not a bank, a fund house, or a licensed advisory. It's a small, self-funded project, not a company: one person writing and checking the math, with no team hiding behind it.

Rather than asking you to trust a name, the goal is to earn trust through transparency — every assumption behind every number is stated inline on each calculator page, and every email that comes in through the contact address below gets read and answered by the same person who built the tool.

How the calculators work

Every assumption (inflation rate, expected return, tax rate) is a plain input you control — nothing is hidden inside the math. The methodology behind each tool, including known limitations, is documented alongside the results on each calculator page.

This site does not provide personalised financial advice. It's an educational tool to help you reason about trade-offs before you talk to your bank, a SEBI-registered investment adviser, or a chartered accountant.
