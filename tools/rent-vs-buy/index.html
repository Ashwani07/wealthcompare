Rent vs Buy in India: The True Financial Equation

In Indian metros, residential rental yields sit at a low 2.5% to 3.2%, while home loan interest rates hover between 7.5% and 8.5%. This means renting a home requires far less monthly cash than buying it.

To compare fairly, we assume the renter invests the exact down payment on day one into equity mutual funds, plus every rupee saved each month between the buyer's EMI + maintenance and the rent. Over 20 years, escalating rent gradually catches up to fixed EMIs, while property appreciation and equity compounding determine the ultimate winner.
