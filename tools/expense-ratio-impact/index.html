Direct vs Regular: Why a 1% Fee Destroys Huge Wealth

A mutual fund's Total Expense Ratio (TER) is not a one-time fee on your monthly instalment. It is an ongoing annual levy subtracted every calendar day from your entire accumulated portfolio.

When you hold a regular plan, the fund house deducts 0.75% to 1.25% extra every year to pay trail commissions to the bank or broker who sold you the fund. Because this deducted money never gets a chance to compound, the cumulative wealth lost over 15 to 25 years can equal hundreds of months of your own contributions.
