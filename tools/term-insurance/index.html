Why the "10x Income Rule" can dangerously under-insure you

The Flaw in the 10x Rule

Insurance agents frequently recommend buying "10 times your annual salary". If you earn ₹25 lakh/year, 10x suggests a ₹2.5 crore cover. However, if you have a ₹70 lakh home loan, two young children whose higher education will cost ₹1 crore after inflation, and an elderly dependent, a ₹2.5 crore cover leaves your family insolvent within 7–8 years.

The Needs-Based Formula

Additional Term Cover Needed = (Income Replacement + Debts to Clear + Future Family Goals + Final Expenses) - (Usable Financial Assets + Existing Life Cover).

This ensures your family can immediately retire all outstanding debt, keep their living lifestyle uninterrupted, and meet every child milestone without liquidating their residential roof.

Term Insurance in India: Core Principles

Pure term life insurance is the only insurance product you need for income protection. It has no investment component, no maturity bonuses, and no cashback—which is why it provides massive ₹1–5 Crore covers for a fraction of traditional endowment or ULIP premiums.

Important Guidelines for Indian Buyers:

  • Never buy Return of Premium (TROP): TROP plans charge 2x–3x higher premiums just to refund your nominal payments at age 60. That extra premium invested in a basic index fund compounds into far more wealth than the insurance company refunds.
  • Do not insure beyond retirement age: Whole-life term insurance (up to age 85 or 99) is unnecessary and expensive. By age 60, your children are independent, loans are repaid, and your retirement corpus is accumulated.
  • Opt for Annual Pay over Limited Pay: Limited pay plans (pay for 5–10 years) compress high premiums into your early career. Annual pay ensures that if you achieve financial independence early, you can simply stop paying premiums.
