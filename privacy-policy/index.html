Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

WealthCompare's calculators run entirely in your browser. We do not require an account, and we do not collect or store the numbers you enter into any calculator — loan amounts, SIP values, income, or any other figures stay on your device and are never sent to our servers, because we don't have a server that receives them.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use limited automated tools to understand traffic and keep the site running:

  • Cloudflare (our hosting provider) processes standard web server logs — IP address, browser type, pages visited — as part of serving the site and protecting it from abuse.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics, if enabled, measures aggregate visits and page performance without using cookies or tracking individuals across sites.
  • Google AdSense serves the ads on this site and may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or generally at aboutads.info.

Cookies

We do not set our own cookies for tracking purposes. Third-party cookies set by Google AdSense are governed by Google's Partner Sites policy. You can disable cookies generally through your browser settings, though this may affect ad relevance and some site functionality.

Your rights

Since we don't collect or store personal data or your calculator inputs on our servers, there is generally no personal data of yours for us to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions about data collected by Google or Cloudflare on our behalf, their respective privacy policies (linked above) explain your options, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where applicable.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
