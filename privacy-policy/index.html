Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Overview

WealthCompare's calculators run in your browser. There is no account to create. The numbers you enter, such as loan amounts, SIP values, goals and incomes, are not sent to us and are not stored by us. They stay on your device, because the site has no server that receives them.

When you click "Export to Excel", the file is created inside your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Who this site is for

WealthCompare is made for people in India. The calculators use Indian rupees and Indian tax and lending rules, and this policy is written for visitors in India.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use a few automated services to keep the site running and to understand how it is used:

  • Cloudflare (our hosting provider) processes standard web server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages visited, as part of serving the site and protecting it from abuse.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics measures overall visits and page performance without using cookies or tracking individuals across sites.
  • Google AdSense may serve ads on this site and may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads and to measure how they perform. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or more generally at aboutads.info.

Other services your browser contacts

To show a page, your browser fetches some files from other companies. Google Fonts supplies the typeface. jsDelivr, a public content delivery network, supplies the chart and spreadsheet-export scripts used on the calculators. When your browser asks for these files, those providers receive standard request details such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies. Google's is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Cookies

We do not set our own cookies for tracking. Third-party cookies set by Google AdSense are governed by Google's Partner Sites policy. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings, though this may affect how relevant the ads are and how some of the site works.

If you email us

If you write to the address below, we use your email address and your message only to read and reply to you.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data or your calculator inputs on our own servers, there is generally nothing of yours for us to access, correct or delete, apart from any email you send us. If you have questions about data collected by Google or Cloudflare, their privacy policies explain your options, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where it applies.

Children's privacy

This site is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above.
